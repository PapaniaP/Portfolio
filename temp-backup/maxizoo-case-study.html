Maxi Zoo Rewards

UX Case Study

Role

UX/UI Designer

Duration

8 weeks

Tools

Figma, Maze, Miro

Overview

Project Brief

Maxi Zoo, a leading pet retailer, needed a digital loyalty program to enhance customer engagement and retention. This case study explores the process of creating a user-centered rewards system that seamlessly integrates with customers' shopping experience.

Problem Statement

Challenge

How might we create a digital loyalty program that encourages repeat purchases while providing genuine value to pet owners and their pets?

Research

User Interviews

Conducted 12 interviews with pet owners to understand their shopping habits and loyalty program preferences.

Competitive Analysis

Analyzed 5 competitor loyalty programs to identify opportunities and best practices.

Survey

Collected data from 150 Maxi Zoo customers about their shopping behavior and reward preferences.

Key Findings

01

86% of pet owners prefer personalized rewards based on their pet's needs

02

73% want to track their rewards progress in real-time

03

92% would increase their shopping frequency with birthday rewards for their pets
